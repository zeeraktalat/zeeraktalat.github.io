Since you are here on this page, you probably want to get in touch with me for some reason. Similarly to many other academics, I am overwhelmed with e-mail, and taking the below into account will maximise your chance of getting a response.

<h5>Do's and Dont's of e-mail</h5>
<ul>
    <li><emph>Do not</emph> send me an e-mail that you've used any generative AI tool to write. No, not even for proof reading. I will not respond to any e-mails that are AI generated.</li>
    <li>If you are a prospective student (Ph.D., or M.Sc.) at the university of Edinburgh, send a research proposal along with your e-mail. This will help me actully tell if there is an overlap in interests.</li>
    <li>If you are seeking a remote internship at the University of Edinburgh: Don't e-mail me. The University of Edinburgh doesn't allow remote internships.</li>
    <li>If you are seeking an in-person internship and are looking for funded positions: Do not e-mail me. Any funded positions of that sort would be advertised elsewhere.</li>
    <li>If you are looking to do a research visit, have funding sorted (or a plan to sort it), then send me a brief research outline for what you'd want to be working on.</li>
    <li>If you are already a student at the University of Edinburgh and you want to chat about a self-proposed honours, master's, or research project, send me an e-mail with an outline of the idea.</li>
    <li>If you are already a student at the University of Edinburgh and you want to chat about research in general, send me an e-mail describing what you want to chat about.</li>
</ul>

I cannot emphasise this enough: Do not send me an e-mail for which generative AI has played a role in drafting. I do not care if you used agents to validate anything, if you only used it to summarise research papers, or if you had it automatically generate the e-mail or just used it for proof-reading it. If I get the impression that an e-mail is generated, I will stop reading it and not respond. Put bluntly: I will not spend the time required to read and respond, if you could not be bothered to spend the time to think and draft.
